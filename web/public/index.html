How it works

  1. Upload PDF CVs here, or drop them into the inbox/incoming folder.
  2. n8n extracts the text and the Gemini agent scores it against the job ad.
  3. A code rule (not the AI) sets the decision from the score.
  4. For interview candidates n8n writes 5 tailored questions and an invitation draft.
  5. You approve or reject. Nothing is sent to a candidate without your approval.
